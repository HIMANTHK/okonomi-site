Your data, kept safe and kept yours

A restaurant system holds your sales, your guests' phone numbers and your staff's hours. Here is how we look after them, in plain words.

Stored in India, backed up daily

Your data lives on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, on a database that runs in two data centres at once. Backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days.

Walled off from every other restaurant

Each restaurant's data is separated inside the database itself as well as by the app, so a bug in the app alone can't show one restaurant another's orders.

Guest details encrypted

Guest phone numbers, emails and allergies are encrypted before they are stored. You can export a guest's data or delete it when they ask, and inactive guest data is removed after 36 months.

Staff see only their part

Every person signs in with their own PIN and role. Cashiers can't refund without a manager, an accountant can read reports but not change anything, and a manager tied to one outlet can't open another. PINs lock after repeated wrong tries.

Two-step sign-in for owners

Owners and managers can require a code from an authenticator app when signing in with a password or pairing a new tablet. A lost tablet can be revoked and wiped from Settings.

Card numbers never reach us

Card payments run through your own payment company's terminal or checkout. Okonomi stores only the reference, and the money settles straight to your bank.

A record of every sensitive action

Voids, refunds, discounts, permission changes and data exports are logged with who did them and when. The log can't be edited.

Offline without losing anything

Bills made offline are stored on the tablet and sent in order when the connection returns. Each is recorded once, even if it's sent twice.

Found a security problem?

Tell us at info@okonomi.in. We reply within two working days and keep you posted until it's fixed.
