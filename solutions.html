Built for the way your kitchen runs

A cloud kitchen and a fine-dining room need different things from the same system. Here's what each kind of restaurant uses, and the plan that usually fits.

Cafés and bakeriesCloud kitchensDine-in restaurants Quick serviceBarsGroups of outlets

Cafés and bakeries

Short queues, lots of add-ons, and regulars who order the same thing every morning.

  • A quick counter with add-ons like oat milk or an extra shot
  • Breakfast items that switch on and off with the time of day
  • Regulars recognised by phone number, with one-tap repeat
  • UPI QR at the counter and your own ordering page
Starter, or Growth with loyalty

Cloud kitchens

No dining room, several brands, and every order coming through an app.

  • Swiggy and Zomato orders straight into the kitchen
  • Prices per app, and a channel pause when the kitchen is full
  • Prep times stretched automatically during a spike
  • Food cost per dish, net of each app's commission
Starter, or Pro for food cost

Dine-in restaurants

Tables, courses, a kitchen team, and guests who book ahead.

  • A live floor plan shared by the host and servers
  • Courses held and fired, allergies confirmed by the kitchen
  • Bookings with deposits, reminders and pre-orders
  • Split bills by seat or item, tips pooled by hours
Growth

Quick service and food courts

Speed at the counter and a clear call-out when food is ready.

  • Combo meals that send each part to its own station
  • A pickup screen showing preparing and ready order numbers
  • Kitchen tickets printed if there's no kitchen screen
  • Billing that keeps going through a network outage
Starter or Growth

Bars and lounges

Open tabs, late nights and staff who share tips.

  • Tabs held on a card and closed with the tip
  • Alerts for checks left open too long
  • Manager PIN for voids and comps
  • Tip pool and payroll export at the end of the week
Growth, or Pro for payroll

Groups of outlets

One owner, several kitchens, and managers who should only see their own.

  • Outlets compared side by side
  • Area managers limited to the outlets they run
  • Purchase orders drafted automatically from par levels
  • Rotas from each outlet's sales pattern
