Billing and payments

A fast counter for QSR, and a check that follows the table for dine-in. Money goes into your own account; card numbers never touch Okonomi.

  • GST-ready bills with CGST and SGST split, GSTIN printed
  • UPI QR on the screen with the amount filled in, card terminals, cash with change
  • Split by seat, by item or evenly; part cash, part card
  • Tips, with a pool shared by hours worked
  • Bar tabs held on a card and closed with the tip
  • Voids, discounts above your limit and refunds need a manager's PIN
  • Cash drawer shifts with X and Z reports

Kitchen display

Tickets go to the station that cooks them. The grill sees grill items, the bar sees drinks. Nothing gets lost on a spike.

  • Tickets change colour at 75% and 100% of the target time
  • Allergy tickets must be acknowledged before they can be cleared
  • Combo meals send each part to its own station
  • Hold mains until the table finishes starters, then fire with one tap
  • Recall a ticket bumped by mistake
  • An all-day count per dish for batch prep
  • No screen yet? Tickets print on the counter printer instead

  • Table states from seated to cleaning, with timers

Stock and food cost

Recipes turn every sale into stock used, so you see what each plate costs and where stock disappears.

  • Recipes with wastage, sub-recipes (gravies, doughs) and size changes
  • Stock drops with every sale, including add-ons like extra cheese
  • Sold-out dishes switch off on every channel automatically
  • Par levels, including different pars for weekends
  • Purchase orders drafted from par; short deliveries raise a credit note
  • Variance: what you used against what recipes say you should have
  • Stock counts with a barcode scanner, and expiry alerts per batch
