No installation visit and no new counter. Sign up on the tablet you already have, bring your menu, and hand out PINs.

Setting up

A short wizard walks you through it. We'll do it with you on a call if you'd rather.

  1. Create your account

    Restaurant name, your mobile number and a password. The tablet you sign up on is ready to bill, and you get your own PIN.

  2. Bring your menu

    Upload the spreadsheet you already have. Set different prices for dine-in, takeaway, Swiggy and Zomato if you use them.

  3. Lay out your floor

    Say how many tables and seats. Drag them into place later if the map matters to you.

  4. Hand out PINs

    Each person gets a 4-digit PIN and a role, and sees only what that role needs.

Switch the Wi-Fi off. Keep billing.

Bills, payments and table changes are saved on the tablet first, then sent up in the order they happened. If the same bill is sent twice, it's counted once.

  • Cash and card work offline. You set the limit for card payments taken without a connection.
  • Kitchen tickets keep reaching kitchen screens through a small in-store box on your Wi-Fi.
  • If two tablets change the same table offline, the latest change wins, and anything that needs a look goes to the manager.

Try it: add a dish, charge it, switch the Wi-Fi off, charge another, then switch it back on.

Switching from another system

You don't have to stop trading to move over. Most restaurants run both side by side for a day and then switch.

Your menu

Export it as a spreadsheet from your current system and upload it. Items with the same name update instead of duplicating.

Your delivery apps

We connect your Swiggy and Zomato stores during setup. Your ratings and listings stay as they are.

Your payments

Connect your own Razorpay account in Settings. Money keeps settling to your bank as before.

Your team

Help when you need it

Service doesn't keep office hours, so neither do we for outages.

How do I reach support?

Email support@okonomi.in any time. Outages that stop you billing or cooking are answered around the clock.

Do you help with setup?

Yes. Book a demo and we'll load your menu, set up tables and staff, and walk your team through a service on a call.

What if a tablet is lost or stolen?

Revoke it from Settings. It's signed out straight away and its local data is wiped the next time it connects.

Will updates interrupt service?

Updates arrive in the background and apply at a quiet moment, without signing anyone out.
